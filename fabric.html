Nestled in Japan's pristine nature, Therman Wellness Resort delivers an unparalleled high-end retreat focused on mind-body rejuvenation, bespoke healing therapies, and refined Japanese hospitality.

THERMAN TEXTILES

THERMAN textiles begin with natural fibres chosen for how they feel against the body. Slowly spun, woven and finished, they become towels, bedding and clothing designed to bring softness into the everyday
Each piece carries the quiet, restorative character of THERMAN beyond the stay..

TEXTURES FOR EVERYDAY REST

Towels, bedding, throws and cushions are created with different weights, weaves and levels of softness. Each texture is considered in relation to the way it touches, surrounds or supports the body.

Together, they form a collection designed around comfort in its many forms.

COMFORT HELD CLOSE

A gentle weight helps the body settle. Softly woven blankets bring warmth without heaviness, creating a quiet layer of comfort for reading, resting or simply remaining still.

The feeling is familiar from the rooms of THERMAN, now made to follow you home.

SHAPED BY THE LANDSCAPE

The colours and textures of the collection are drawn from the world surrounding THERMAN—
pale grasses, warm earth, weathered stone and the soft light of changing seasons.
Muted and natural, each textile settles into a space without disturbing its sense of calm.

MATERIALS WITH A MEMORY

Natural fibres retain a character of their own. Small variations in texture, tone and surface are embraced rather than removed, allowing each piece to feel quietly individual.

With use, the fabric softens and becomes more familiar—holding the memory of the body and the life lived around it

CLOSE TO ITS ORIGIN

The collection begins with materials connected to the natural world. Fibres are selected for warmth, breathability and the way they change gracefully through time.

Their origin remains present in every surface—creating textiles that feel grounded, tactile and naturally at ease.

MADE TO MOVE NATURALLY

THERMAN clothing is designed to move with the body rather than shape it. Relaxed silhouettes, soft structures and generous proportions create freedom from unnecessary restriction.

Made for quiet mornings, open air and days allowed to unfold at their own pace.

SOFTNESS, WORN

The warmth experienced within THERMAN becomes something that can be worn. Tactile knits and relaxed layers surround the body with comfort while maintaining a clean, considered form.

Clothing becomes another way to carry a sense of shelter through the day.

ROOM TO BREATHE

Ease begins with space around the body. Soft lines, light volume and breathable textures allow each garment to feel comfortable across movement and rest.

Nothing holds too closely or asks the body to perform. The clothes simply follow its natural rhythm.

LAYERS OF WARMTH

Different textures and weights can be layered as the temperature and body change. Each piece is designed to sit comfortably with the next, creating warmth that feels adaptable rather than excessive.

A quiet wardrobe shaped around season, sensation and personal ease.

EASE WITHOUT EXCESS

Clean silhouettes and understated details allow the material to remain at the centre. The collection feels refined without becoming formal, and comfortable without losing its sense of structure.

These are clothes for feeling settled—in the room, in the landscape and within yourself.

DESIGNED AROUND THE BODY

Proportion is considered through movement: the fall of a shoulder,
the width of a sleeve and the space created with every step.

Each garment is shaped to feel intuitive from the moment it is worn, bringing physical ease into the smallest movements of the day.

A QUIETER WAY TO DRESS

A restrained palette and softened forms make each piece easy to return to.
Garments can be worn together or layered with what already belongs to you.

Simple, versatile and tactile, the collection reduces the distance
between dressing and feeling at ease.

THE FEELING, CONTINUED

The experience of THERMAN does not have to remain within its rooms. Worn against the body or placed throughout the home, each textile carries forward the same sense of softness, warmth and unhurried comfort.
A lasting part of the stay, made for the life that continues beyond it.
