GAMASOTE

At the heart of GAMASOTE is the gamasot, a traditional Korean iron cauldron. Chicken is simmered slowly over steady heat,
allowing the broth to develop a deep, clear flavour that cannot be rushed.

RICE, MADE FRESH

Rice is prepared in small quantities and brought to the table while still warm. Each grain holds its natural texture and gentle sweetness, offering a quiet balance to the depth of the broth.

Simple, familiar and made with the same attention as the dish it accompanies.

DEPTH, DRAWN SLOWLY

The broth develops gradually as the ingredients simmer over a steady heat. Time draws out their natural richness, creating a flavour that feels full and comforting without relying on excess seasoning. Every ladle carries the warmth and depth gathered within the cauldron.

MADE TOGETHER

Kimchi is prepared in-house to accompany the gentle richness of the chicken broth. Crisp cabbage, spice and fermentation bring brightness and contrast to each spoonful.

It is not simply served beside the meal, but considered as part of its complete balance.

A COMPLETE KOREAN TABLE

GAMASOTE is served as a complete Korean meal: clear chicken broth, tender meat, warm rice and a thoughtful selection of seasonal side dishes.

Each element has its own character, yet together they create a table that feels nourishing, balanced and deeply familiar.

ONE MORE EXPRESSION

The depth developed in the gamasot continues beyond a single bowl. Served with noodles, its warmth takes on a different texture—lighter in rhythm, yet grounded in the same slow preparation.

Another expression of GAMASOTE, shaped by simplicity and the comfort of Korean food.
